Validation Rules
  1. Procurement case must be approved and active.
  2. Order number must be unique.
  3. Order date cannot precede sanction/EAS date.
  4. Completion due date must be on or after start date.
  5. Presiding Officer, Member 1 and Member 2 are mandatory.
  6. The same person cannot occupy multiple committee roles.
  7. Presiding Officer seniority should be checked when rank information is available.
  8. Only active personnel are available in the personnel dropdowns.
  9. At least one Terms of Reference is required.
  10. Approved orders must not be overwritten; amendments should use a new version.
